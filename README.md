## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hello! I'm **Guoji Gan (甘国基)**. My research interests center on **computer vision**, particularly **human pose estimation** and **human motion understanding**.

I'm interested in how machines perceive human body structure, understand motion across time, and learn meaningful representations from visual information.

- 🔬 **Research Area:** Computer Vision
- 🧍 **Primary Focus:** Human Pose Estimation & Human Motion Analysis
- 🎥 **Related Interests:** Video Understanding, Action Recognition, and Visual Representation Learning
- 🌱 **Goal:** Explore robust and effective approaches to understanding human pose and movement

## 🔍 Research Interests

- **Human Pose Estimation:** 2D/3D human pose estimation, keypoint localization, and pose representations
- **Human Motion Understanding:** skeleton-based action recognition, spatio-temporal modeling, and motion analysis
- **Video Understanding:** fine-grained human actions and temporal information in videos
- **Multimodal Visual Learning:** combining RGB appearance with pose and motion cues

## 📖 Topics I'm Exploring

- How to represent human body structure and motion effectively
- How to model spatial relationships between joints and temporal changes across frames
- How to combine visual features and pose information for better human-centric understanding

## 🏷️ Research Keywords

`computer vision` · `human pose estimation` · `2D/3D pose` · `human motion analysis` · `skeleton-based action recognition` · `video understanding` · `spatio-temporal modeling` · `RGB + pose`
